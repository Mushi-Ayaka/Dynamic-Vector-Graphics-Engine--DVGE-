# Ecosystem and Plugin Policy - Ember Motion Studio

This policy regulates the development, distribution, and ownership of extensions and add-ons (hereinafter "Plugins") for Ember Motion Studio.

## 1. Intellectual Property of Plugins

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, **Jonatan Barón** reserves the intellectual property and exploitation rights of all plugins developed for the official Ember Motion Studio ecosystem.

Any third-party contributions to the official plugin repository or the engine core imply the necessary assignment of rights so that Jonatan Barón can distribute, modify, and manage said extensions as an integral part of Ember Motion Studio and the DVGE engine.

## 2. Third-Party Responsibility

Ember Motion Studio allows for the loading of external plugins. Jonatan Barón is not responsible for damages, data loss, or system failures caused by third-party developed plugins or unofficial modifications to the engine.

## 3. Commercial Use

The commercial use of Ember Motion Studio and its engine is permitted under the MIT license, but the use of specific plugins from the official ecosystem may be subject to additional terms defined by the author.

## 4. Security

The development of plugins containing malicious code, performing unauthorized telemetry, or compromising the security of the host system is prohibited. Jonatan Barón reserves the right to revoke access or remove any plugin that violates these rules.
