# User Manual: Ember Motion Studio v5.9.0

## The 5-Step Master Workflow

Ember v5.9.0 introduces an optimized workflow to ensure professional results on the first attempt:

1.  **Canvas Configuration**: Set your project's dimensions, duration, and FPS.
2.  **Artifact Management**: Inject images, videos, or databases (Excel/CSV). It is crucial to add detailed titles and descriptions; this is the information the AI will use to understand how to manipulate each resource.
3.  **AI Context Builder**: Generate the technical rule manual (PDF) and drag it to your favorite AI chat (Claude, GPT, or **DeepSeek in Expert Mode**).
4.  **Visual Refinement**: Once the code is generated, adjust values in the **Inspector** in real-time to polish aesthetic details.
5.  **Export & Iteration**: Render your final video or return to the Builder to include current code and request modifications from the AI.

---

## 1. Plugin Catalog

Expand your graphics library with a single click:
- Access the **Plugin Catalog** from the home screen.
- Install or update professional templates directly from the official repository.
- Installed plugins will automatically appear in your project template list.

---

## 2. The Studio (Workspace)

### 2.1 AI Context Builder (Smart Knowledge)
Located at the bottom of the control panel. It allows you to "teach" the AI about your current project. You can choose to inject the context of your artifacts, dimensions, or even the current code.

### 2.2 Inspector & Artifact Linking
You can now link inspector fields directly to your loaded artifacts (e.g., selecting an image from your gallery as a layer background).

### 2.3 Smart Tips (Reminders)
If you are new, you will see a reminder about the **Quick Guide**. This modal becomes transparent when hovering over it so as not to obstruct your view while working.

---

## 3. Broadcast Export

The **Ember** rendering engine exports high-fidelity files:
- **ProRes 4444**: Maximum quality with real transparency for broadcast.
- **H.264 / MP4**: Ideal for social media (no transparency).
- **GIF**: For fast previews and stickers.

:::tip[Tip for DaVinci Resolve]
If the ProRes video appears with a black background: Right-click on the clip -> **Clip Attributes** -> **Alpha Mode** -> **Straight**.
:::

(Updated for Ember v5.9.0 Stable)
